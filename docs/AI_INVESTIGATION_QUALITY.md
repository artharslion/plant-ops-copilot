# AI investigation and work-order evidence quality

## Evidence semantics

`investigation-extractive-v7` retains the immutable authorized evidence ledger and its hashes. An alarm includes its historical trigger, threshold snapshot, recovery state and timestamp. Trigger lookup uses the effective date, point context and exact timestamp. Raw event excerpts use two bounded two-minute queries around the trigger and recovery anchors; they do not scan the interval between those anchors. Anchor samples are selected before surrounding samples. Expired raw records do not erase the alarm snapshot or surviving minute aggregates.

Completed UTC minute windows cover two minutes before through three minutes after each event anchor, capped at the start of the investigation's current minute. Trigger and recovery in the same minute share a window; distant anchors remain separate. A distinct five-minute investigation-current window cannot substitute for the historical event. Event-anchor buckets are prioritized over surrounding buckets; the latest completed bucket is prioritized in the current window. An unfinished event minute is explicitly excluded from minute aggregation and can be represented only by available raw evidence.

Quality entries identify the point, half-open time interval, type, and minute-level overlap with the alarm interval. Empty/unavailable aggregates, gap flags, Bad/Uncertain counts, time anomalies, and window boundaries have separate meanings. Boundary entries locate flags in the first/last minute; they do not claim a gap at the exact window edge without raw evidence. Missing quality never independently establishes a sensor or communication fault. Sampling configuration, constant values and KeepAlive are not claims about physical sample rate or device faults.

## Authorized reference material

The initial document query uses the actual asset code, point code and quantity plus investigation intent terms. Up to six currently published authorized chunks are collected. Complete parser chunks (up to the parser's existing limit) replace the former 150-character snippets; titles include version, section and line range. Existing plant/asset/role access, published-version checks, chunk hashes, expansion and approval revalidation remain in force.

The model receives whole facts packed into a bounded evidence payload, with `omittedFactCount` disclosing facts omitted for input capacity, never missing database records. Core observations, raw anchors, quality/scope and maintenance facts take priority over long document chunks. Expanded budgets allow up to 16,000 UTF-8 evidence bytes; smaller budgets start at 8,000 bytes and adapt downward after including the actual schema/tool/prompt framing and protected observations. The complete ledger and retrieval timestamps remain available for human expansion; repeated per-item retrieval timestamps are omitted from the model projection. A partly supplied item may be returned again by a subsequent read. Large evidence sets are not guaranteed to fit every chunk or follow-up round.

`INDUSTRIALSIM_P101_DEMO_MANUAL.md` is software simulation reference material tied to IndustrialSim commit `3d5e7c0` and the observed workbench rule configuration. It does not disclose scenario injection timing or a predetermined root-cause answer, and is not a real equipment manufacturer's authorized procedure. `deploy/Publish-IndustrialSimManual.ps1` checks the rule configuration, publishes through authenticated document APIs, verifies authorized retrieval/expansion hashes and preserves the database AI selection. It never requests a model completion.

## Output and proposal contract

Version `investigation-extractive-v7.1` distinguishes an explicitly delimited conditional antecedent in a check's escalation from an assertion that data is currently missing. Only that antecedent is excluded from availability contradiction checks; its consequence and subsequent sentences remain checked. Observation/reference, numeric advice and control restrictions are unchanged. The failed 2026-10-26 run identified `check-escalation-contradicts-raw-evidence`; its rejected provider text was not retained, so the exact original wording cannot be recovered from that diagnostic alone.

The Docker workbench enables `AI_SERVER_RENDERED_FACTS=true`. The model submits a draft with empty observations and the fixed safe summary; the server first validates the draft's original references, rejects unexpected model-authored observations, then assembles up to eight observations and a summary of up to three complete observation sentences from the authorized ledger. Facts are not paraphrased. Trigger, recovery and the separate current window remain mandatory; repeated unqueryable trigger/recovery statements retain both references. When the newest completed minute is unavailable, the current representative is the latest available minute, with the unavailable interval still disclosed. The compatible static wire schema and public InvestigationResult.v1 remain unchanged. Library defaults retain model-rendered facts for compatibility; server-rendered mode is an explicit profile setting.

Up to two hypotheses and six checks are permitted. A hypothesis is optional and must have a verbatim observed basis, limitations and a distinguishing verification; manuals, data-quality warnings and unqueryable-data statements alone cannot establish a cause. Each check has `record`, a verifiable completion condition, conditional `escalation` and authorized references. A server-rendered report with no checks is rejected; a task status such as “未完成/部分完成” is not a completion condition. Missing-data claims are checked against the full ledger for known raw/maintenance records. Available excerpts do not prove a complete physical history. Known automatic collection scope and explicitly unqueryable raw history are retained as server-supplied limitations even if the model returns no missing-information entries. Invalid model claims are not silently removed to manufacture success. Historical v1 JSON remains readable without rewriting old reports.

Numeric advice is accepted only when its numeric content is inside complete facts from that item's cited evidence. Matching an isolated number or device identifier is insufficient. Remaining advice cannot introduce numeric parameters or control/setting commands. References and textual entailment checks do not establish physical root cause or field authority; human review remains necessary.

Output allowance is the run's remaining budget, rather than a fixed per-round cap. Docker uses 32,000 input and 6,000 output tokens, a 600-second whole-run deadline and a 120-second stream-idle/header wait; see the provider setup for transport semantics. Library fallback limits remain 16,000/2,000 and 180 seconds. Lower configured limits, four rounds and eight tools remain enforced. Reported usage releases only the completed attempt's reservation; unknown usage retains it. Compact prompts preserve the documented tool-then-answer regression within its original 16,000-token cumulative budget. Diagnostics record dispatch stages/counters and the specific invalid field/category without provider text. Truncation, incomplete content, invalid references and transport failures remain failures. Historical failed answers are not reconstructed or automatically retried.

Proposal drafts contain object/event context, ordered checks, the evidence to record, source section references and completion/escalation conditions. They link the full investigation rather than copying every observation and hypothesis. Quality warnings are deduplicated; at most eight appear in the draft, with remaining entries retained in the investigation. Existing payload/version hashes, proposal expiry, human confirmation, transactional execution, plant access checks, audit trails and work-order authorization remain unchanged. Creating a draft does not execute it or change an alarm.

Do not enable live model test switches without separate billing authorization. Regression coverage includes delayed/recovered events, retained aggregates after raw cleanup, UTC minute boundaries, quality localization, large complete manual chunks under budget, grounded numeric advice, hypothesis limitations, authorization changes, proposal approval races and immutable investigation snapshots.
